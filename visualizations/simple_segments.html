Simple DNA Segment Plot

Paste your segments below or keep the sample. Each item should have: chr (1-22), start, stop, and optional label and color. Example shown uses a few sample rows.

Segments JSON

Tips:
  • Colors are optional; omit color to auto-assign by label.
  • To group by person, keep label consistent; each unique label gets a color in the legend.
  • Coordinates should be base-pair positions on GRCh37/38 to match your source; Ideogram supports either.
